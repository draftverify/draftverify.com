# Personnel Competence & Training Requirements

<p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#6b7280;">

## 1. Purpose

Organizations within certification scope shall ensure that personnel performing DraftVerify-controlled tasks are competent for those tasks.

## 2. Required knowledge

Applicable personnel shall understand, at a level appropriate to their role:

- NA identity pathway;
- physical identifiers;
- verification-before-connection workflow;
- mismatch response;
- change control;
- incident escalation;
- record responsibilities; and
- limits of their technical authority.

## 3. Role-based training

Training shall be appropriate to assigned responsibility rather than identical for every employee.

DraftVerify may define role profiles for:

- brewery activation personnel;
- distributor handling personnel;
- venue operators;
- managers;
- technicians;
- assessors; and
- certification decision personnel.

## 4. Assessment of competence

Competence may be demonstrated through:

- knowledge assessment;
- observed task performance;
- supervised implementation;
- prior relevant qualification; or
- another documented method accepted by the applicable program.

## 5. Training completion records

Where required, training records shall identify:

- person;
- role;
- module or competency;
- completion date;
- assessment result where applicable; and
- renewal or review date where applicable.

## 6. Training credentials

DraftVerify may issue a **training completion credential**.

A training credential confirms completion of the stated training only.

It shall not be represented as:

- venue certification;
- brewery certification;
- distributor certification;
- professional trade licensing;
- regulatory qualification; or
- authority to perform regulated technical work.

## 7. Refresh and retraining

Retraining or competency review shall occur when required by the applicable program, including where material procedures change, repeated errors occur, or the person's role changes materially.

## 8. Certification relationship

Organization certification under F-32 may require specified personnel competence evidence.

Employee training completion alone does not establish organization certification.
