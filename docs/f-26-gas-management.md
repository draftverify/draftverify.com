# Gas System Interface & Safety Control

<p style="font-size:0.95rem;color:#6b7280;">
Version 2.0 · Publication Date: 2026-09-22 · Status: Published / Effective

> **Safety boundary.** DraftVerify does not set universal dispense pressures, gas blends, cylinder rules, regulator service intervals, or pressure-vessel requirements. Those values and procedures shall come from the beverage producer, equipment manufacturer, qualified technician, and applicable law or code.

## 1. Purpose

F-26 ensures that gas-system configuration does not undermine the identity, traceability, or verified configuration of an in-scope NA draft pathway.

## 2. Qualified configuration

Gas type, blend, operating pressure, regulator configuration, check-valve requirements, and component suitability shall be determined by competent personnel using applicable external requirements.

DraftVerify certification shall not be represented as certification of pressure-system engineering or gas safety.

## 3. Identification

Where gas configuration is material to correct operation of an in-scope NA product, the organization shall maintain sufficient identification to determine:

- applicable gas source;
- product or line served;
- relevant regulator or control point; and
- current approved configuration.

## 4. Change control

A material gas-system change affecting an in-scope pathway shall be controlled under F-27 and documented before the affected NA pathway is returned to verified service.

## 5. Unsafe or uncertain condition

If personnel identify a suspected leak, damaged pressure component, unknown gas source, unknown regulator setting, or other potentially unsafe condition:

- service shall be stopped where required by the applicable safety procedure;
- the condition shall be escalated to qualified personnel; and
- DraftVerify verification shall not be used as authority to perform technical repair.

## 6. Records

Where required by the certification scope, service records shall identify:

- date;
- affected system or line;
- person or service organization;
- material configuration change; and
- re-verification status.

## 7. Certification effect

A gas-related condition is a DraftVerify nonconformity when it:

- makes the intended NA pathway uncertain;
- causes registry or physical configuration to disagree;
- prevents required verification; or
- remains unresolved after a material system change.

A separate safety hazard may also require action under external law or qualified technical procedures independent of DraftVerify.
