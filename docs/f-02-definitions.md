# Definitions & Normative Language

> **Document control.** Definitions in this document apply throughout the F-Series unless a document expressly defines a term differently for a limited scope.

## 1. Normative verbal forms

**shall** — expresses a requirement necessary for conformity.  
**shall not** — expresses a prohibition necessary for conformity.  
**should / should not** — expresses a recommendation.  
**may / need not** — expresses permission.  
**can / cannot** — expresses possibility or capability.  
**must / must not** — reserved for obligations or constraints originating outside DraftVerify, such as law, regulation, manufacturer instructions, or physical necessity.

## 2. Core definitions

### 2.1 Applicant
Organization or person formally seeking DraftVerify certification or another documented conformity decision.

### 2.2 Certification
A written DraftVerify conformity decision authorizing a defined certification status for a defined scope, subject to F-32.

### 2.3 Certification Mark
A DraftVerify-controlled sign, badge, seal, wording, or other identifier whose use indicates a defined DraftVerify certification status. Registration status is jurisdiction-specific; the symbol **®** shall not be used unless registration exists for the relevant mark and jurisdiction.

### 2.4 Certificate
The controlled record issued by DraftVerify identifying the holder, scope, applicable Standard version, status, and certificate identifier.

### 2.5 Conformity
Fulfilment of applicable DraftVerify requirements within a defined scope.

### 2.6 Conformity assessment
A structured evaluation of evidence against applicable DraftVerify requirements.

### 2.7 Critical nonconformity
A failure that defeats a core identity, verification, integrity, safety, or evidence control and requires an adverse conformity decision until corrected.

### 2.8 DraftVerify Registry
The controlled digital record system used for applicable identity, verification, certificate, and status records.

### 2.9 DTI
DraftVerify Tag Identifier; a unique controlled identifier associated with a DraftVerify record.

### 2.10 Identity pathway
The defined relationship through which NA product identity is maintained from **keg → coupler → line → faucet**.

### 2.11 Non-alcoholic / NA
A category designation used within DraftVerify for products represented by their producer or operator as non-alcoholic under the law and product requirements applicable to the relevant jurisdiction. DraftVerify does not establish a universal legal ABV threshold.

### 2.12 Nonconformity
Failure to satisfy an applicable DraftVerify requirement.

### 2.13 Scope
The sites, systems, products, services, activities, or organizational units covered by a conformity assessment or certificate.

### 2.14 Verification
A documented check that specified identity information or system configuration matches the expected controlled state.

### 2.15 Verification event
A recorded verification action containing the fields required by F-30.

## 3. Certification claim terminology

The following expressions imply a DraftVerify conformity decision when used in relation to a third-party organization, site, system, product, or service:

- DraftVerify Certified;
- DraftVerify Approved;
- DraftVerify Verified;
- Certified to the DraftVerify Standard;
- equivalent wording reasonably implying that DraftVerify itself has assessed and approved conformity.

Such claims require active written authorization under F-32.

A factual statement such as “our internal procedures reference the DraftVerify Standard” does not by itself constitute certification, provided it is accurate and does not imply DraftVerify endorsement.

## 4. Regulatory terminology

**DraftVerify requirement** means a private requirement of the DraftVerify Standard.

**Legal requirement** means an obligation imposed by applicable law or regulation.

The two terms shall not be treated as interchangeable.

## 5. Interpretation

Defined terms shall be interpreted consistently across the F-Series. Where ambiguity affects a certification decision, DraftVerify shall document the interpretation used and shall apply it consistently to materially similar cases until the underlying text is clarified through F-40 governance.
