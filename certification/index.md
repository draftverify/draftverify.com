<section class="section section-rule">
  <div class="container split-layout">
    <div>
      <div class="kicker">Certification mark licence</div>
      <h2>Certification controls the claim.</h2>
      <p>
        An active certificate does not transfer ownership of DraftVerify marks. Authorized mark use is
        limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, scope-specific, and revocable under F-32 and the applicable written licence.
      </p>
      <p>
        A party may accurately say it has referenced the public Standard without certification. It may not
        imply that DraftVerify has certified, approved, verified, endorsed, or audited it unless that status is actually active.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="standards-note">
      <div class="record-label">Registry rule</div>
      <p>
        A copied certificate or badge does not establish active status. For DraftVerify purposes,
        the current Registry status controls.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
