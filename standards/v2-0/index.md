    <h2>Major changes from v1.0</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Established F-1 as the controlling normative master standard.</li>
      <li>Adopted controlled normative language: shall, should, may, and can.</li>
      <li>Established the DraftVerify Certification Program and scope-specific conformity decisions.</li>
      <li>Separated certification, training completion, and implementation support.</li>
      <li>Established critical conformity gates that cannot be offset by scoring.</li>
      <li>Made Registry certificate status controlling for DraftVerify purposes.</li>
      <li>Established limited, scope-specific certification-mark licensing.</li>
      <li>Clarified that DraftVerify is private and voluntary, not law or regulatory accreditation.</li>
      <li>Clarified copyright boundaries and separated copyright from trademark, certification, contract, and software rights.</li>
      <li>Removed universal engineering and legal claims outside DraftVerify's proper identity-and-verification scope.</li>
      <li>Strengthened assessor competence, evidence, impartiality, appeals, and governance controls.</li>
      <li>Established controlled publication, interpretation, and rights-management rules in F-40.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Superseded edition</h2>
    <p>
      DraftVerify Standard v1.0 is superseded for new certification decisions. Historical copies do not override
      current v2.0 requirements or current Registry status.
    </p>
