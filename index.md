<section class="section">
  <div class="container split-layout">
    <div>
      <div class="kicker">The operational problem</div>
      <h2>Draft hardware does not identify intent.</h2>
      <p>
        A stainless keg, coupler, beer line, and faucet do not inherently tell an operator whether a
        pathway is intended for alcoholic or non-alcoholic product. In shared draft environments,
        configuration changes, keg rotations, and staff turnover can make that identity harder to follow.
      </p>
      <p>
        DraftVerify creates a defined identity layer so the intended NA pathway can be followed from the
        cold room to the point of service.
      </p>
    </div>
    <figure class="system-photo">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/draftverify-hero-keg-room.png' | relative_url }}"
           alt="Draft system equipment in a keg room">
      <figcaption>Identity is applied to existing draft infrastructure rather than requiring a new draft system.</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </div>
</section>
