<section class="section">
  <div class="container split-layout">
    <div>
      <div class="kicker">The control problem</div>
      <h2>Draft hardware does not identify intent.</h2>
      <p>
        Kegs, couplers, beverage lines, and faucets do not inherently identify whether a pathway is
        intended for alcoholic or non-alcoholic product. DraftVerify applies a controlled identity layer
        so the intended NA pathway can be followed, verified, and recorded.
      </p>
      <p>
        Version 2.0 separates three things deliberately: the <strong>published requirements</strong>,
        the <strong>conformity decision</strong>, and the <strong>licensed certification claim</strong>.
      </p>
    </div>

      <h2>Certification is a decision, not a sticker.</h2>
      <p>
        DraftVerify Certification is issued only for a defined scope after applicable requirements
